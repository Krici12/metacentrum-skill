The CERIT-SC Kubernetes platform and Rancher UI are available to all users who accept the following terms and conditions.

## [Terms and conditions](#terms-and-conditions)

### [Usage rules](#usage-rules)

Access to the Kubernetes platform, Rancher UI, and other related web services is granted free of charge only to members of academic or non-commercial institutions in the Czech Republic with research and/or education objectives. If you are interested in using our infrastructure for commercial research, please contact us to discuss whether that research project can be conducted within e-INFRA CZ.

These services are available on a “best effort” basis. The operator strives to maintain service availability and security, with essential services running in high-availability mode and automatically monitored. Support is available during normal working hours.

For the full description of the service, see the [full text of the terms of use](https://www.metacentrum.cz/en/about/rules/).

### [Account renewal](#account-renewal)

User accounts expire annually on February 2. By the end of December or beginning of January, users are notified to request account renewal.

If the account is not renewed by February 2, it expires. The data, login, and username still exist, however, and can be reactivated upon request. After a four-year grace period, the expired account is deleted together with all its data.

## [Account](#account)

A MetaCentrum account is required to use this service, unless a special exception is negotiated.

### [How to apply for the first time](#how-to-apply-for-the-first-time)

-

Follow the instructions at the [registration form](https://metavo.metacentrum.cz/en/application/form).

-

You can check the state of your application on the [Perun pages](https://metavo.metacentrum.cz/en/application/statecheck).

For general information, see the MetaVO pages concerning [application](https://metavo.metacentrum.cz/en/application/index.html) and [account management](https://metavo.metacentrum.cz/en/myaccount/index.html).
